What it uses

The hour, the day of the week, the month, and whether the day is one of six quiet public holidays (New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas). If you add a weather forecast, it uses that too; the weather changes the forecast only a little.

What it cannot know

  • Accidents, road closures and heavy snow: these cause its largest errors.
  • Unusual days that are not public holidays, such as the day after Thanksgiving.
  • Anything that changed after 2018, when its data ends. It covers one road in one direction.
